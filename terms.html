YouTube

The "TIS uploader" tool uses YouTube API Services. Anyone using it agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and how TIS handles data is described in our Privacy Policy.

Content

Every clip is a short excerpt from a public Twitch stream, posted with credit to the streamer and a link to their channel, and only from creators who allow their streams to be clipped. Creators can ask for any clip to be removed or credited differently by messaging the Today in Streams Page, and we will act on it promptly.

The tools may only be used to publish, schedule and manage TIS's own uploads and to read their statistics. They may not be used to access other people's accounts or data. Access can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page or in Facebook's business integrations settings.
