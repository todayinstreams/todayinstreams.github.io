Privacy Policy

Today in Streams publishing tools · Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy covers the private tools used by the team behind Today in Streams (TIS) to publish its own clips: the Meta app used for the Today in Streams Facebook Page, and the Google app "TIS uploader" used for the Today in Streams YouTube channel.

What the tools do

They are private tools for the owner of Today in Streams. They:

  • upload short video clips with their titles, captions and cover images to our own Facebook Page and our own YouTube channel;
  • schedule those posts;
  • read our own posts' statistics (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time) so we can see which clips people enjoy.

The tools are not offered to the public. Nobody else signs in to them, and they do not post to any other Page, channel, profile or group.

Information we access

  • From visitors, viewers and followers: nothing. The tools do not collect, store or share personal information about people who view, like, comment on, subscribe to or follow our accounts.
  • From our own accounts: only our own uploads and their total statistics, provided by Meta and by YouTube through their official APIs. These are numbers per post, not data about individual people.
  • Google user data: the "TIS uploader" app signs in only to the owner's own Google account, to upload, schedule and manage videos on the Today in Streams YouTube channel (YouTube Data API) and to read that channel's own analytics (YouTube Analytics API).
  • Access tokens: the sign-in tokens are kept privately on the owner's own computer. They are never shared, sold or sent to anyone other than Meta and Google.

How we use it

Only to publish our own clips to our own accounts and to understand how those clips perform. We do not sell, rent or share any data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Today in Streams' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

Our YouTube tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page.

Data retention and deletion

The tools keep no personal data about anyone. Post statistics are kept only as long as they are useful for planning content. To ask a question or request deletion of any data related to you, send a message to the Today in Streams Page on Facebook. We will reply and delete anything related to you within 30 days.

Credit to creators

Every clip credits the original streamer by name with a link to their channel. If you are a creator and want a clip removed, message us and we will take it down promptly.

Terms

Use of the tools is also covered by our Terms of Service.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same link with a new date.
